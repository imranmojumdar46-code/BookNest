❓ Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account to browse books?

No. Anyone can browse, search and view book details. An account is only needed to add or manage books.

How do I list a book?

Register, log in, then open your Dashboard and press “Add book”. Fill in the details and optionally upload a cover.

Can I edit or delete other people's books?

No. Only the member who listed a book can edit or delete it.

Is my password safe?

Yes. Passwords are never stored as plain text; they are hashed before being saved in the database.

Which images can I upload?

PNG, JPG, GIF or WEBP files up to 2 MB.

How do I switch to dark mode?

Use the 🌙 button in the top bar. Your choice is remembered on this device.

How can I contact you?

Use the Contact page. Messages are stored securely in our database.
